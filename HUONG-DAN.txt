THU MUC WEBSITE "DAU AN DI SAN QUANG TRI"

Cac tep va thu muc:
  index.html         Trang web. Thuong khong can sua.
  videos.js          Danh sach video. Do cong cu them-video.html tao ra.
  them-video.html    Cong cu co nut bam de khai bao video. Nhap dup de mo.
  videos\            Noi tha video, phu de, anh bia cua tung di tich.
    ben-pha-gianh\   ... va 7 thu muc khac, moi di tich mot thu muc.
    MAU-phu-de.vtt   Tep mau de xem dinh dang phu de.

CACH THEM MOT VIDEO (4 buoc)
1. Chon noi luu video:
   - YouTube (khuyen dung): tai video len YouTube, che do "Khong cong khai".
   - Google Drive: tai video len Drive, chia se "Bat ky ai co duong lien ket".
   - Tep trong thu muc: tha tep .mp4 vao thu muc videos\ten-di-tich (chi hop voi video ngan, nhe).
2. Nhap dup them-video.html, chon di tich, dan dia chi (hoac ten tep), bam "Them vao danh sach".
3. Bam "Tai videos.js ve may", roi chep tep videos.js moi de len tep videos.js cu trong thu muc website.
4. Tai ca thu muc website len mang lai (Netlify keo tha lai, hoac GitHub Upload files).
